         9. MODULE 1 – VIDEO COURSE GENERATOR
         "Topic to Multi-Chapter Educational Video in One Click"

The Video Course Generator transforms a single text topic into a complete multi-chapter educational video course. The entire pipeline — from course structure to the final rendered video — is fully automated.

Pipeline: Topic → AI Course Layout → Slide Generation → TTS Narration → Caption Sync → AI Image Generation → Remotion Video Render

STEP 1 – COURSE LAYOUT GENERATION
User enters a topic (e.g., "Blockchain Technology"). The /api/generate-course-layout endpoint validates input using Zod schema (generateCourseLayoutSchema) and sends a structured prompt to Groq LLM via the AI Fallback Client. The AI generates a course structure as JSON with multiple chapters, learning objectives, and estimated durations. Stored in the coursesTable.

STEP 2 – SLIDE CONTENT GENERATION
For each chapter, Groq LLM generates rich HTML slide content including titles, bullet points, definitions, and Reveal.js compatible presentation data. Each slide stored in chapterContentSlides with unique slideId and narration JSON.

STEP 3 – TEXT-TO-SPEECH NARRATION (Enhanced TTS Pipeline)
The lib/enhanced-tts.ts pipeline provides:
• Text Sanitization — Strips HTML, converts smart quotes, removes zero-width Unicode, fixes punctuation
• Smart Chunking — Sentence-boundary detection, 2400-char max per chunk, comma/semicolon splitting for oversized sentences
• Retry with Exponential Backoff — 3 retries, 2000ms initial delay, 10000ms max, 2x multiplier
• Sarvam AI API — Model: Bulbul v3, sample rate: 22050 Hz, WAV output, configurable pace (1.05x) and temperature (0.6)
• WAV File Merging — Strips 44-byte headers from chunks, concatenates raw PCM data, builds new RIFF/WAVE header
Audio uploaded to Appwrite Storage.

STEP 4 – WORD-LEVEL CAPTION SYNCHRONIZATION
Duration distributed across words based on character length. Timestamps stored in captions JSON field. These drive the Remotion caption overlay.

STEP 5 – AI IMAGE GENERATION (Leonardo AI)
LLM generates descriptive photorealistic prompts. Leonardo AI (lib/leonardo.ts — 44KB) generates 1024×576 widescreen images with parallel generation support. Images uploaded to Appwrite Storage.

STEP 6 – REMOTION VIDEO COMPOSITION
Remotion (remotion/Composition.tsx — 40KB) assembles chapters with professional transitions, background images, synchronized audio, and Hormozi-style dynamic captions (lib/caption-styles.ts — 14KB) with word-by-word highlight. Final MP4 rendered server-side.

         10. MODULE 2 – SHORT VIDEO GENERATOR
         "1-Click Viral Short-Form Video Factory"

Produces viral short-form videos (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) through a fully automated 7-step Inngest durable workflow.

Pipeline: Niche → Script → Voice → Captions → Images → Video Conversion → Render

CREATION WIZARD (6 Steps):
Step 1 — Niche Selection (e.g., "Ancient Civilizations")
Step 2 — Voice & Language (Sarvam AI voices, multi-language)
Step 3 — Background Music (cinematic, upbeat, dramatic, chill)
Step 4 — Video Style (Realistic, Cyberpunk, GTA, Anime, Watercolor, Dark Fantasy)
Step 5 — Caption Style (Hormozi-style presets from caption-styles.ts)
Step 6 — Series Details (title, duration, platform, publish time)

INNGEST DURABLE WORKFLOW (inngest/functions.ts — 126KB):

Step 1 – Fetch Series Data from DB, determine voice/language/caption style.

Step 2 – AI Script Generation: Groq LLM generates 6-scene script (310-350 words). 3 attempts with temperature escalation (0.7→0.8→0.85). Each scene has narration, imagePrompt, videoPrompt, sceneCategory. Anti-repetition rules enforced. Non-English scripts translated via lib/translate.ts.

Step 3 – TTS Voice Generation: Per-scene audio via Sarvam AI. Per-scene pace and temperature settings. Smart chunking + rate limiting. WAV merging. Duration calculated from WAV header.

Step 4 – Caption Synchronization: Word-level timestamps distributed proportionally across scenes.

Step 5 – AI Image Generation: Leonardo AI parallel generation. One image per scene from imagePrompt.

Step 5.25 – Image-to-Video Conversion: Kling AI converts static images to 5-second video clips. Custom MP4 box parser for duration probing (reads mvhd atom, no ffprobe needed).

Step 5.5 – Avatar Clip Injection: 6 pre-recorded avatar clips (3 intros, 3 outros) with pairing rules. FFmpeg atempo filter chain for time-stretching TTS to match video duration.

Step 6 – Remotion Video Render: Assembles all assets into final MP4.

Step 7 – Status Update: Marks series as "completed". Failure handler resets stuck assets.

         12. MODULE 4 – MIGOO STUDIO (DIRECTOR'S CHAIR)
         "Full Creative Control — You're the Director, AI is Your Crew"

The most advanced module — gives users full creative control over every aspect of video production while reusing 100% of the existing Inngest pipeline infrastructure.

DOCUMENT SOURCE SYSTEM:
Upload PDFs, ZIPs, or images → Sarvam Vision OCR extracts text as contextMarkdown → AI generates script from extracted content → enables "Document-to-Video" conversion.

SCENE ASSET MANAGER (Per-scene control):
• 'ai_image' — Let Leonardo AI generate the scene image (default)
• 'user_upload' — Inject your own photo or video clip
• 'kling_video' — Use Kling AI image-to-video conversion
• 'doc_image' — Use an image extracted from the source document

Mixed media example:
Scene 1: Company logo (user_upload) → Scene 2: AI visualization (ai_image) → Scene 3: Chart from PDF (doc_image) → Scene 4: Product demo video (user_upload) → Scene 5: AI cinematic video (kling_video) → Scene 6: Team photo (user_upload)

HUMAN TOUCH SCORE:
Gamified script editor that scores how much the user has personalized AI-generated scripts. Encourages authentic, engaging content that doesn't sound robotic.

PER-SCENE VOICE CONFIGURATION:
Each scene can have different pace (0.5–2.0) and temperature (0.0–1.0). Slow dramatic pace for intros, normal for body, energetic for climax.

INTEGRATION WITH INNGEST PIPELINE:
Studio passes a studioPayload to the existing generateShortVideo function:
• scriptData bypasses AI script generation (Step 2 skipped)
• sceneAssets override AI images per scene (Step 5 customized)
• voice/language override series defaults (Step 3 customized)
• captionStyle override (Step 4 customized)
No code duplication — excellent software engineering.

         13. MODULE 5 – AI NOTES GENERATOR
         "Smart Notes, Beautifully Crafted"

Converts any source material into beautifully formatted, multi-page, printer-ready study notes with 6 styles and 4 page designs.

Pipeline: Source Input → Sarvam Vision (if doc) → Smart Sampling → Groq LLM → Structured JSON → A4 Renderer → PDF/PNG Export

SOURCE INPUT SYSTEM (Auto-detected):
• Topic mode — Just a title, AI generates from knowledge
• Text mode — Paste raw content (any length, smart sampling handles it)
• Document mode — Upload PDF/images, Sarvam Vision OCR extracts text

TIERED MODEL STRATEGY (lib/chunked-notes-generator.ts):

Tier 1 — llama-3.3-70b-versatile: 16K max chars, 5.5K output tokens, high quality
Tier 2 — llama-3.1-8b-instant: 55K max chars, 6.5K output tokens, high capacity
Automatic escalation: If Tier 1 hits 413/429, catches TOO_LARGE error, escalates to Tier 2 transparently.

SMART CONTENT SAMPLER:
• Heading Detection — Identifies #headers, numbered lines, ALL CAPS, Title Case
• Section-Based Sampling — Head (2.5–5K chars) + section excerpts + Tail (1.2–2.5K chars)
• Uniform Fallback — 20 evenly-spaced samples if no headings found
• Multi-key rotation — Comma-separated GROQ_API_KEYS with automatic cycling

6 NOTE STYLES:
Cornell (two-column study), Mind Map (visual hierarchy), Flashcards (term→definition), Infographic (visual stats), Cheat Sheet (dense reference), Timeline (chronological).

4 PAGE DESIGNS:
Botanical (sage green organic), Abstract Pastel (pink blobs), Geo Pebbles (colorful hand-drawn), Elegant Leaf (gold framed, navy accents).

A4 MULTI-PAGE RENDERER (app/notes/[noteId]/page.tsx — 1515+ lines):
A4-compliant dimensions, automatic pagination, print-ready CSS, PDF/PNG export via html-to-image + jsPDF, decorative backgrounds per page, professional typography.
